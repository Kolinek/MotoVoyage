Your routes, on the map

Moto Voyage helps you plan motorcycle routes on OpenStreetMap, save them for later, and open turn-by-turn navigation in Google Maps — including multi-stop trips.

Download for Android Terms & user guidelines

Plan on the map

Tap the map to add waypoints. The app builds a road route along roads (OSRM).

Save & load

Name your route and save it. Open the Saved tab to load or delete routes anytime.

Navigate in Google Maps

Send the full route — including all stops — to Google Maps for voice-guided riding.

Undo & clear

Remove the last point with Undo, or clear everything and start fresh.

Inside the app

Purpose-built for planning and riding

Every screen is tuned for quick decisions on the road: clear controls, sensible defaults, and no clutter between you and your next ride.

01

Map — sketch the ride, refine the line

Drop points on the map and Moto Voyage snaps a sensible road route between them using open routing data. Name the route, undo mistakes, or clear and start over without losing your flow.

  • Tap to add waypoints — the route updates as you build your loop or one-way trip.
  • Clear & Navigation — reset the canvas or hand off the full path to Google Maps when you are ready to ride.
  • Undo & Save route — step back one point at a time, then persist the finished line under a name you choose.
  • Map / Saved tabs — stay in planning mode or jump to your library without digging through menus.
02

Saved routes — your garage, on the phone

Everything you save stays on your device for quick recall. Load a route back onto the map for edits, send it straight to navigation, or remove it when you no longer need it — with a confirmation so nothing disappears by accident.

  • Load — restores the route to the map so you can tweak or ride it again.
  • Navigation — opens the same multi-stop itinerary in Google Maps for voice-guided riding.
  • Safe delete — the red control removes a route only after you confirm, matching the app’s careful tone.
  • Focused top bar — “Saved routes” keeps context obvious when you leave the map.
